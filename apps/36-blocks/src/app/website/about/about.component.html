OUR APPROACH

Clarity is
a product feature.

36blocks brings application authentication, people, and investigation into an internal workspace for our teams.

Developers should be able to answer a few practical questions without assembling the story across disconnected screens: what is connected, who has access, what happened, and what to do next.

Start with the outcome

Configure a block, connect your application, and verify that sign-in works.

Keep context close

Keep users, their application, and recorded operator actions connected.

Respect boundaries

Keep tenant administration separate from platform operations. Your application owns its resource permissions.

Our focus today is the verified capabilities of the current product. Broader identity infrastructure for applications and AI agents remains a future direction.
