Security at 36Blocks

Understand the access boundaries and configuration that protect your connected applications.

Authentication configuration

Blocks support configurable login methods, provider settings, session expiry, and hCaptcha integration. Availability and enforcement depend on the selected block and deployment. Configure the providers and verify failure, expiry, and recovery behavior for your application before launch.

Identity and application access

Authentication identifies a user. It does not grant access to every application or organization. Roles and permissions help model membership, while each connected product remains responsible for resource ownership and business authorization on its backend.

Do not rely on a browser callback or hidden UI control to protect a resource. Your application must verify authentication results, expiry, membership, and permissions before granting access.

Investigation and activity history

Request logs help investigate API failures. Sensitive-operation activity history records the operations that have been instrumented, including Login as attempts. Coverage and availability depend on the deployed version and permissions.

Confirm retention, operator access, and the required investigation coverage with the team. Use activity records within their documented coverage.

Deployment responsibilities

Transport protection, database encryption, backups, recovery, and availability depend on the hosting environment. Confirm these controls and any service commitments for your deployment. Ask the team for environment-specific availability and recovery commitments.

Report a security concern

Contact the team through the contact page. Include the affected flow and safe reproduction steps. Do not include passwords, session tokens, or personal data in your initial message.
